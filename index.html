02 — Projects

001

Indian Railways Arrival Delay Prediction

An end-to-end machine learning pipeline on one month of real Indian Railways running data (1.28M stop-level records, 3,892 trains, 4,736 stations) that predicts, at any stop, how late a train will reach its final destination. Data checks exposed that the source reports missing arrivals as "on time": after a stop 2+ hours late, an exact 0 was about 50× more common than a real recovery of that size, so 47,603 fake zeros (3.7%) were flagged and treated as missing. Evaluation uses a date-based split and leakage-safe, out-of-fold history features. Predicting the correction to a historical baseline, rather than the delay itself, was the biggest gain, beating tuning and a neural network. On unseen dates the final model reaches an MAE of 15.5 minutes, 38% better than persistence and 19% better than the history rule, with 75.7% of predictions within ±10 minutes.

002

RAG-Based Regulatory Compliance Chatbot

A retrieval-augmented assistant over 27 regulatory documents covering 25 Indian and international frameworks (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PMLA, DPDP Act, IT Act, GDPR, ISO 27001), where every claim carries a framework, section, and page citation traceable back to the source PDF. A confidently wrong answer traced to the ingest layer, not the prompt, led to owning the whole PDF-to-index pipeline: gazette schedules get their own sections, unruled tables are rebuilt row by row, and documents are split at their own clause numbering into 6,533 clauses, so each chunk is one citable obligation. Retrieval runs Cohere embeddings, a cosine-distance floor, Cohere reranking, and small-to-big expansion that widens each matched clause with its neighbours so the model reads a rule with its provisos. Out-of-corpus questions are refused at zero LLM cost instead of being answered from model memory. A 30-case harness needing no model calls measures Hit@1 of 96%, MRR 0.962, and 4/4 correct refusals.

004

CNN-based Steganalysis Using Modified-AlexNet

A modified AlexNet CNN for image steganalysis, adapting the architecture to classify images as cover (clean) versus stego (containing hidden embedded data). The baseline was augmented with a high-pass noise-residual preprocessing layer and average-pooling stages to amplify the weak steganographic signal, improving sensitivity to subtle pixel-level embedding. Trained and evaluated on the BOSSbase benchmark of 10,000 grayscale images, reaching 65.25% detection accuracy.

Python3 TensorFlow CNNs NumPy BOSSbase

005

Signature-Based Steganalysis and Feature-Augmented Password Cracking for SteganographX Plus

A signature-based steganalysis engine written in C that detects BMP images tampered by SteganographX Plus via its byte-level marker, paired with an LSB extraction routine that recovers hidden payloads directly from stego pixel data. A Feature-Augmented Password Cracking (FAPC) module exploits the signature and the tool's PRNG properties to recover its protection password and unlock concealed data, cutting brute-force time drastically while preserving full detection accuracy.

C GCC GDB Image Processing SteganographX Plus

05 — Research & Publications
Published Paper  ·  Springer Feb 2026

Signature-Based Steganalysis and Feature-Augmented Password Cracking for Steganography Tool SteganographX Plus

Sayak Bhowal, Bitanu Biswas, Madhusudan Paul

This paper analyzes SteganographX Plus, identifying a fixed signature (an embedded email ID) via chosen-stego/message attacks and reverse-engineering its PRNG-based XOR encryption. The authors propose Feature-Augmented Password Cracking (FAPC), exploiting signature and PRNG properties to drastically cut brute-force password-cracking time versus traditional methods, while preserving full detection accuracy.

DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-15398-2_12
